Chapter Four

The Pastor's Office

Pastor Mike's office smelled like coffee and certainty, which Dave suspected were the two load-bearing substances of the entire operation. He had made the appointment under the pretense of "spiritual questions," which was true the way "some assembly required" was true of a jet engine.

"Dave," Pastor Mike said, in the voice he used for hospital visits. "How's Sarah? How are things at the office?"

"I ate a burger on Tuesday," Dave said, "and I haven't been able to stop being alive since."

There was a pause of the kind that reorganizes a room. Pastor Mike set down his mug with the care of a man defusing something. Dave watched him reach for the shelf of answers he had been stocking for thirty years, and watched him find — visibly, for the first time in Dave's presence — that the shelf was further away than he remembered.
